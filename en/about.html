Our Story

Bulut Cold Storage Facility

Serving the food industry since 2009, Bulut Cold Storage Facility has become one of Turkey's leading cold storage operators with modern cooling technology and years of experience.

Since our establishment, we have prioritized customer satisfaction and work to store food products under optimal conditions. We make a difference in the industry with our modern technological infrastructure, experienced team, and quality-focused service approach.

We continue to add value to our customers with our hygiene standards, uninterrupted cold chain, and reliable service approach.
