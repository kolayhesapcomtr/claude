What Products Do We Store?

As Bulut Cold Storage Facility, we meet all cold chain needs in the food industry. We preserve the quality of your products with special temperature and humidity control for each product group.

🥩

Meat Products

Red meat, white meat, and processed meat products

Storage Conditions

Temperature: -18°C
Humidity: 85-90%
Maximum Duration: 12 Months

Meat Products We Store

  • Red Meat (Beef, Lamb, Mutton)
  • White Meat (Chicken, Turkey)
  • Processed Meat Products (Sausage, Salami, Hot Dog)
  • Ham and Pastrami
  • Frozen Doner Kebab
  • Grill and BBQ Products

Seafood
