Cookie Policy

Information About Cookies Used on Our Website

Last Updated: November 17, 2024

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (such as computer, tablet, or mobile phone) by the website you visit. Cookies are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a more secure experience, deliver better user experiences, and analyze website performance.

2. Types of Cookies

Cookies used on our website are categorized based on their purposes:

2.1. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function properly. Without these cookies, the website cannot work correctly.

  • Session Cookies: Active during your visit and deleted when you close your browser
  • Security Cookies: Used to enable security features
  • Language Preference Cookies: Remember your chosen language preference

2.2. Performance Cookies

These cookies are used to measure and improve website performance.

  • Track visitor numbers
  • Determine which pages are more popular
  • Understand how users navigate the site
  • Error detection and correction

2.3. Functionality Cookies

These cookies enable the website to provide enhanced features and personalization.

  • Remember your preferences (language, region)
  • Store personal settings like font size and theme
  • Recognize your previous visits

2.4. Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how our website is being used.

  • Google Analytics cookies
  • Visitor behavior analysis
  • Traffic source tracking
  • Page view statistics

4. How to Control Cookies

You can control and manage cookies using your browser settings. Most browsers are set to automatically accept cookies, but you can change these settings.

Cookie Settings in Popular Browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies

Important Note: If you completely disable cookies, some features of our website may not work properly and your user experience may be negatively affected.
